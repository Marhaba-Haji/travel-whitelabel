

# Facelyft Technology Landing Page

## Overview
A professional, corporate landing page for Facelyft Technology and Marketing Solutions – showcasing your complete whitelabel B2B travel portal solution with a clean blue & white color scheme that conveys trust and reliability.

---

## Page Sections

### 1. Hero Section
- **Headline**: Emphasizing the complete travel tech solution
- **Subheadline**: All-in-one B2B agent portal with flights, hotels, visas & more
- **Pricing highlight**: Rs. 18,799/year prominent badge
- **Multiple CTAs**: "Request Demo", "Get Started", "Contact Sales"
- **Hero visual**: Professional illustration or mockup of the platform

### 2. Trusted By / Stats Bar
- Key statistics (agencies served, bookings processed, etc.)
- Trust indicators and credibility badges

### 3. Complete Features Section
Showcase all API integrations in an elegant grid:
- ✈️ **Flight API** – Real-time flight bookings
- 🏨 **Hotel API** – Global hotel inventory
- 📋 **Visa API** – Visa processing system
- 🎯 **Activities API** – Tours & experiences
- 🏷️ **Own Branding** – Custom domain & white-label

### 4. Multi-Portal System
Visual showcase of the 4 portal types:
- **Admin Portal** – Manage packages (Holiday, Hajj, Umrah, Visas, Transport, Guides)
- **Supplier Portal** – Give content access to suppliers
- **Agent Portal (B2B)** – For your travel agent network
- **B2C Portal** – Direct customer bookings

### 5. How It Works
Simple 3-4 step visual process:
1. Sign up & choose your plan
2. Connect your domain & branding
3. Add your content & suppliers
4. Start selling to agents & customers

### 6. Pricing Section
- Clear pricing card: Rs. 18,799/year
- Feature list highlighting what's included
- "No hidden fees" messaging
- CTA: "Start Your Free Trial"

### 7. Testimonials
- Placeholder testimonial cards from travel agencies
- Star ratings and agency logos

### 8. FAQ Section
- Collapsible accordion with common questions
- Topics: Setup, APIs, Support, Customization

### 9. Footer with Contact
- Contact form for inquiries
- WhatsApp/phone number
- Email and social links
- Quick navigation links

---

## Design Elements
- **Colors**: Professional blue (#1e40af) with white backgrounds, subtle gray accents
- **Typography**: Clean, modern fonts (system fonts for performance)
- **Icons**: Lucide icons for consistent visual language
- **Animations**: Subtle fade-in effects on scroll
- **Responsive**: Fully mobile-optimized design
- **Components**: Cards, badges, buttons with hover effects

---

## Technical Notes
- This will be a static landing page (no backend required initially)
- Contact form will show a toast notification (can be connected to email later)
- Smooth scroll navigation between sections
- Optimized for conversion with strategic CTA placement
